🚀 **Excited to share my latest End-to-End Data Science Project: Telco Customer Segmentation & Churn Analytics!**

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges facing the telecom industry today, where acquiring a new customer is up to 25x more expensive than retaining an existing one. To address this, I built a production-ready solution that combines unsupervised and supervised machine learning:

🔍 **What I did:**
1. **Customer Segmentation**: Used **K-Means Clustering** on tenure, billing charges, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) to profile 7,000+ customers into four operational segments (VIP, Loyal, New, and At-Risk).
2. **Churn Prediction**: Trained and optimized **Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost** models. **XGBoost** emerged as the champion model with **80.24% accuracy**.
3. **Interactive Dashboard**: Designed a premium dashboard using **Streamlit** and **Plotly** to visualize data, query the customer database, and predict customer churn in real-time.
4. **Explainability & Financial ROI**: Integrated local explainability (XAI) to highlight risk factors and built a business calculator to quantify **Revenue at Risk ($)** and evaluate discount-campaign ROI.

💡 **Key Takeaway**: 
Month-to-month contracts and Fiber Optic services are the top churn drivers, while Tech Support and Online Security attachments act as critical customer retention anchors. Upgrading month-to-month users to annual plans yields a projected **10.8x ROI** on retention marketing spend.

## 🌐 3. Personal Portfolio Website Description

### **Telco Customer Analytics & Churn Sandbox**
*An end-to-end customer analytics solution bridging machine learning and business outcomes.*

* **The Problem**: Telecom companies experience high subscriber attrition due to intense competitor campaigns, leading to high revenue loss.
* **The Solution**: An interactive analytics platform. K-Means clustering defines customer segments (VIP, Loyal, New, At-Risk), while an XGBoost classifier predicts individual customer churn.
* **The Value**: The app translates model probability metrics directly into financial indicators—identifying monthly and annual revenue at risk—and explains the classification drivers (local explainability).
